Proposal

Proposed vocabulary

A question gets an answer. The answer rests on findings. Each finding has sources. You check them. Nothing on screen needs a word outside that sentence.

Left out

  • No "asked by", "needed by" or "decision" fields. That is project tracking.
  • No dashboard. Home is one list: what to check, then the answers you already have.
  • No matrix view, no charts, no questions that repeat on a schedule.
  • No new data. Everything shown comes from records the app already stores.

Check what your AI tells you

You ask your AI why a number moved, and it gives you an answer. This app keeps what it found, where each finding came from, and which ones you have checked yourself.

  1. Ask your AI a question. It saves what it finds here.
  2. Check each finding against its source. Mark the ones you have checked.
